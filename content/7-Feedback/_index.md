### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The environment at AWS Vietnam is highly professional and dynamic. The modern workspace at the Bitexco Financial Tower provides immense creative inspiration for interns. Members of the FCJ team are always enthusiastic and create the best conditions for us to access pioneering Cloud technologies.

**2. Support from Mentors / Team Admins**  
I deeply appreciate the support from the mentors. Despite their busy schedules, they always dedicated time to provide guidance and answer technical questions. Mentors encouraged me to research and solve problems independently, which helped me grow much faster.

**3. Alignment between Work and Major**  
The internship tasks were perfectly aligned with my Computer Science major at university. Working on Serverless and Generative AI projects allowed me to directly apply foundational knowledge to the real-world environment of a leading technology corporation.

**4. Opportunities for Learning & Skill Development**  
During the internship, I had the chance to master core services such as AWS Amplify, Lambda, and especially Amazon Bedrock. My Node.js and Python programming skills have significantly improved after completing the main project.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**  
The working culture here is very open and respectful. However, regarding team coordination in projects, there was sometimes a significant gap in skills and proactivity among members. During certain phases, I put in extra effort to carry the majority of the workload to ensure the project stayed on schedule and met AWS's high-quality standards. While demanding, this was a valuable opportunity to practice working under pressure and enhance my independent problem-solving skills.

**6. Policies / Benefits for Interns**  
The support policies and internal training programs are very well-structured. Being able to participate in major events like Community Day is a fantastic privilege that helped expand my professional network.

### Other Questions
- **What were you most satisfied with?** Being able to directly deploy applications on real Cloud infrastructure and learning system design thinking from top experts.
- **What should the company improve for future interns?** There should be a more rigorous competency assessment when dividing internship groups to ensure a balanced skill level, preventing excessive pressure from falling on a single individual.
- **Would you recommend this internship to friends?** Definitely yes, as this is the best environment to break your limits and shape your career in the tech industry.

### Proposals & Desires
- **Proposal:** Organize regular technical sharing sessions (Tech Talks) between different internship groups to exchange practical experiences.
- **Desire:** I hope to have the opportunity to continue contributing to AWS in an official capacity after completing my degree.
- **Other Feedback:** The FCJ team should build a peer-review system for group projects to ensure fairness and encourage highly active members.
