## Overview

In this section, you will go through the steps to delete all the resources created throughout this lab. It is a best practice to remove unused resources to avoid unexpected charges.

---

## Implementation

### Step 1: Open General settings

Go to the AWS Amplify Console:

https://console.aws.amazon.com/amplify/apps

In the left navigation panel of the **ai-recipe-generator** app:

- Select **App settings**  
- Choose **General settings**

---

### Step 2: Delete the app

In the **General settings** section:

- Click **Delete app**

![img](/images/5-Workshop/5.7-Cleanup/img1.png)

Confirm the deletion to complete the cleanup process.
